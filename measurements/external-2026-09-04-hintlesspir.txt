why this shape : db_rows * db_cols = 6,712,448 exactly matches this
              repository's own real record count (229 * 29312, m = 29312 in
              the implementation under measurement). The split between db_rows and
              db_cols is the shape that minimizes total client bytes
              (request scales with db_cols, response with
              num_shards * db_rows) for a fixed record count and record
              width: db_rows = sqrt(N / num_shards) = sqrt(6712448/128) =
              229 exactly, giving db_cols = 29312.
why not 1-byte records : the shipped benchmark and the paper's own tables
              use db_record_bit_size=8 (one shard). This repository's
              records are 128 bytes, and RESULTS.md already flagged that
              scaling num_shards changes the picture (as it did for YPIR
              and VIA before this).
